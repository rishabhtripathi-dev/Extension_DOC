Privacy Policy for OneClarity AI Assistant

Data Collection

OneClarity AI Assistant collects the following data:

  • Webpage URLs you visit
  • Text you select on webpages
  • Page content (first 3000 characters)
  • Questions you ask

Data Usage

This data is used solely to:

  • Provide AI-powered responses to your questions
  • Summarize webpage content
  • Generate insights about text you select

Data Storage

Data is sent to our backend server and Azure OpenAI for processing. We do not store your data permanently. Logs are kept for 30 days for debugging purposes only.

Third-Party Services

We use Azure OpenAI to process your requests. Their privacy policy applies: https://privacy.microsoft.com/
